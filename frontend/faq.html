General Questions

What is ORBIT?
ORBIT (Online Recommendation for Benefits, Insurance & Terms) is a Smart-powered insurance recommendation platform. We help users find the most suitable insurance plans based on their personal profile, preferences, and needs. Our smart algorithm analyzes various factors to provide personalized recommendations from top Indian insurance providers.
Is ORBIT free to use?
Yes, ORBIT is completely free to use! You can create an account, get recommendations, compare plans, and download policy documents at no cost. We earn a commission from insurance providers when you purchase a policy through our platform, so you never pay anything extra.
How does ORBIT recommend insurance plans?
Our smart algorithm analyzes multiple factors to provide personalized recommendations:
  • Your age and life stage
  • Annual income and financial goals
  • Family composition and dependents
  • Existing coverage and insurance needs
  • Risk tolerance and budget preferences
  • Health status and lifestyle factors
Which insurance providers does ORBIT work with?
We partner with 10+ top insurance providers in India, including:
  • Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC)
  • SBI Life Insurance
  • HDFC Life Insurance
  • ICICI Prudential Life Insurance
  • Bajaj Allianz General Insurance
  • Tata AIG General Insurance
  • And many more...

Account & Registration

How do I create an account on ORBIT?
Creating an account is simple:
  • Click the "Sign Up" button on the homepage
  • Fill in your details (name, email, phone, etc.)
  • Create a secure password
  • Verify your email address
  • Start getting recommendations!
Alternatively, you can sign up instantly using your Google account.
Can I sign up using Google?
Yes! We offer Google Sign-In for quick and easy registration. Simply click "Continue with Google" on the login or signup page, select your Google account, and you'll be logged in instantly. Your basic profile information will be automatically filled from your Google account.
I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
To reset your password:
  • Click "Login" on the homepage
  • Click "Forgot Password?" link
  • Enter your registered email address
  • Check your email for the reset link
  • Click the link and create a new password
If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.
How do I update my profile information?
After logging in, click on your profile icon in the navigation bar. From there, you can access your profile settings and update your personal information, contact details, and preferences. Keep your profile updated for more accurate insurance recommendations.
How do I delete my account?
To delete your account, go to Profile Settings and click "Delete Account," or contact our support team. Please note that deleting your account will permanently remove all your data, including saved recommendations and policy documents. This action cannot be undone.

Recommendations & Analysis

How accurate are ORBIT's recommendations?
Our smart algorithm is trained on extensive insurance data and continuously improved based on user feedback. While we strive for high accuracy, recommendations should be used as a starting point for your research. We recommend reviewing plan details carefully and consulting with insurance advisors for complex needs.
Can I compare multiple insurance plans?
Absolutely! ORBIT allows you to compare up to 4 plans side-by-side. You can compare:
  • Premium amounts and payment options
  • Coverage amounts and benefits
  • Policy terms and conditions
  • Claim settlement ratios
  • Additional features and riders
How is my premium calculated?
Premium calculations consider multiple factors:
  • Your age at the time of purchase
  • Coverage amount requested
  • Policy term duration
  • Type of insurance plan
  • Health status and lifestyle habits
  • Any additional riders or benefits
Note: Final premiums are determined by the insurance provider and may vary slightly from our estimates.

Insurance Policies

What types of insurance does ORBIT recommend?
We provide recommendations for various insurance types:
  • Life Insurance: Term plans, endowment, ULIPs, whole life
  • Health Insurance: Individual, family floater, critical illness
  • Motor Insurance: Car and two-wheeler insurance
  • Travel Insurance: Domestic and international coverage
  • Home Insurance: Property and contents protection
Can I purchase insurance directly through ORBIT?
When you select a plan, we redirect you to the insurance provider's official website or connect you with their representative for the final purchase. This ensures you receive the most up-to-date policy terms and complete the KYC process directly with the insurer.
How do I download my policy documents?
After selecting a plan, you can download a detailed policy summary as a PDF from your dashboard. For the actual policy document after purchase, you'll receive it directly from the insurance provider via email or through their customer portal.
What is a claim settlement ratio?
Claim Settlement Ratio (CSR) is the percentage of claims an insurance company settles against the total claims received in a financial year. A higher CSR indicates that the insurer is more likely to approve and pay claims. ORBIT displays CSR for each provider to help you make informed decisions.
